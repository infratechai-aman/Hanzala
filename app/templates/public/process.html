Process

Every project follows the same loop. The loop is the point — finishing is just where the current lap ends.

  1. Problem
  2. Understand
  3. Plan
  4. Build
  5. Test
  6. Break
  7. Debug
  8. Reverse engineer
  9. Understand deeply
  10. Rebuild

The AI workbench

AI tooling is part of the workflow: it increases leverage on drafting, searching, and boilerplate. It does not replace technical responsibility. On every build, the human — me — remains responsible for:

  • directing the work
  • making decisions
  • reviewing output
  • debugging
  • understanding the architecture
  • testing
  • learning

If I cannot explain how something I shipped works, the process failed — no matter how well the output reads.
